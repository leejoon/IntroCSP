Key Characters

Katniss Everdeen

The Girl on Fire / District 12

A fierce, resourceful bowhunter who volunteers for the 74th Hunger Games in place of her younger sister, Prim, inadvertently sparking a national rebellion.

Peeta Mellark

The Baker's Son / District 12

A gentle and strong-willed artist-tribute who uses charm, public speaking, and painting skills to protect Katniss through the games and the war.

Haymitch Abernathy

District 12 Mentor

A cynical, sharp-witted past victor of the 50th Hunger Games (Second Quarter Quell) who guides Katniss and Peeta with unorthodox strategy.

President Coriolanus Snow

Ruler of Panem

The ruthless, rose-loving authoritarian dictator of the Capitol who uses fear, media control, and the Hunger Games to suppress the 12 districts.

3 Fun Facts About the Series

01

An Inspiration From Reality TV

Suzanne Collins got the idea for the story while channel-surfing between reality television shows and footage of the Iraq War, realizing how media could easily blur entertainment and real-life tragedy.

02

Jennifer Lawrence's Hair Transformation

Jennifer Lawrence had to dye her naturally blonde hair dark brown for the role of Katniss and wore a special dark brown wig for parts of the sequels to protect her hair.

03

Massive Global Bestseller

The original trilogy has sold over 100 million copies worldwide and has been translated into more than 50 different languages.
