Origin Story

Shadow the Hedgehog was created by Professor Gerald Robotnik aboard the Space Colony ARK. Designed through advanced genetic research, Shadow became known as the Ultimate Life form

Following tragic events involving Maria Robotnik, Shadow's life became filled with mystery, loss, and redemption. Unlike Sonic, Shadow walks the line between hero and antihero, making difficult choices for what he believes is right.

His calm attitude, incredible power, and darker personality have made him one of the most beloved characters in the Sonic series.
