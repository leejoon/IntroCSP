🍝 Spaghetti Pasta

 

Jerry Pank Cookipedia.co.uk

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • Salt and pepper
 

How to Make It

  1. Boil spaghetti according to package instructions.
  2. Heat olive oil and cook garlic.
  3. Add tomato sauce and seasonings.
  4. Mix in the cooked spaghetti.
  5. Serve warm and enjoy.
 

🥞 Pancakes

 

Nightcrafter (talk | contribs)- wikimedia-commons

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
 

How to Make It

  1. Mix all ingredients in a bowl.
  2. Pour batter onto a hot pan.
  3. Cook until bubbles appear.
  4. Flip and cook the other side.
  5. Serve with syrup or fruit.
 

🥗 Fresh Garden Salad

CC0 - Public Domain license and Royalty free. by Martin Vorel

 

Ingredients

  • Lettuce
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Cucumber
  • Carrots
  • Salad dressing
 

How to Make It

  1. Wash all vegetables.
  2. Chop them into bite-sized pieces.
  3. Place in a large bowl.
  4. Add dressing and toss gently.
  5. Serve fresh.
